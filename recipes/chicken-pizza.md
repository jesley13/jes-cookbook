---
title: Chicken Pizza
---

This makes two 11–12 inch pizzas, enough for 6 adults if served with a side.

1. Pizza dough
500 g bread flour / strong maida
325 ml lukewarm water
7 g instant dry yeast
1 tsp sugar
1½ tsp salt
2 tbsp olive oil

If using ordinary maida, start with about 300 ml water and add more only if needed.

2. Chicken topping
500 g boneless chicken
1 tsp salt
1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
½ tsp black pepper
1 tsp oregano/Italian seasoning
1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
1 tbsp oil
3. Pizza sauce
400 g tomato puree
2 tbsp tomato paste
4–5 cloves garlic, finely chopped
1 tsp oregano
½ tsp chilli flakes
½ tsp black pepper
1 tsp sugar
1 tbsp olive oil
Salt to taste
4. Cheese

Use the Amul Mozzarella + Cheddar blend you showed me.

For 2 pizzas:

250–300 g cheese

I'd use about 275 g.

1. Make the dough

Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt.

Add the water gradually and mix.

Add the olive oil.

Knead for 8–10 minutes until you have a smooth, elastic dough.

It should be soft and slightly tacky, but not wet.

Place in an oiled bowl, cover and leave for 1–1½ hours, until roughly doubled.

Better option

If you have time, put the covered dough in the refrigerator for 12–24 hours.

Take it out about 1½–2 hours before making the pizzas.

The slow fermentation gives you a much better-tasting crust.

2. Prepare the chicken

Cut the chicken into small pieces, roughly 1 cm.

Mix with:

salt
Kashmiri chilli
pepper
oregano
ginger-garlic paste
oil

Heat a pan very hot.

Cook the chicken for 5–7 minutes, until just cooked through and lightly browned.

Don't overcook it — it will cook again in the oven.

Keep aside.

3. Make the pizza sauce

Heat olive oil.

Add garlic and cook for about 30 seconds.

Add:

tomato puree
tomato paste
oregano
chilli flakes
black pepper
sugar
salt

Simmer 15–20 minutes, uncovered.

You want a thick sauce.

This is important — watery sauce makes homemade pizza soggy.

Cool before using.

4. Heat the oven — VERY important

For good pizza, get the oven as hot as it will go.

Ideally:

250–280°C

Put your pizza stone/steel or a heavy baking tray inside while the oven preheats.

Give it at least 30–45 minutes to get properly hot.

If your oven only reaches 230°C, that's okay; we'll adjust the baking time.

5. Shape the pizzas

Divide the dough into two equal pieces.

Make each into a ball.

Rest for 15 minutes.

Stretch each into an approximately 11–12 inch circle.

Don't use a rolling pin if you want a lighter crust.

Press outward from the centre, leaving a slightly thicker rim.

Transfer to baking paper or a well-floured pizza peel/tray.

6. Assemble

For each pizza:

Sauce

Use approximately 100–120 g sauce.

Spread a thin layer.

Don't drown the pizza.

Chicken

Use approximately 250 g chicken.

Spread evenly.

Cheese

Use approximately 135–150 g cheese.

Again, don't create a huge pile of cheese.

A good pizza has balanced toppings, not a mountain of them.

7. Bake

Slide the pizza onto the hot surface.

At 250–280°C:

Bake approximately 7–10 minutes.

At 230°C:

Bake approximately 10–14 minutes.

You want:

browned cheese
crisp bottom
golden crust
slightly charred edges

If your oven has a top/broiler/grill element, switch it on for the final 1–2 minutes to brown the cheese.

Watch it carefully.

⭐ My recommended version for you

Since you've said you prefer avoiding unnecessary vegetables, I'd make this simply:

Pizza dough → tomato sauce → seasoned chicken → Amul mozzarella/cheddar → oregano → chilli flakes.

No capsicum.
No mushrooms.
No onions.

The chicken and cheese become the stars.

One important cheese tip

The Amul cheese blend you showed me should work very well, but because cheddar can release some oil at high heat, don't go much beyond 300 g for these two pizzas.

If you want that classic pizza-shop look, put about 80% of the cheese under/around the chicken and the remaining 20% over the top. That gives you both good coverage and visible browned cheese.
