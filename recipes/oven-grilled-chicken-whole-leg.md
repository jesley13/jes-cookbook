---
title: 🍗 Oven-Grilled Chicken Whole Leg
---

6 whole legs

Assuming each whole leg is roughly 300–350 g, this gives a good dinner for 6.

Ingredients
First marinade
6 whole chicken legs
2 tbsp lemon juice
1½ tsp salt
1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
1 tsp turmeric
1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
Second marinade
150 g thick curd
1½ tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder
1 tsp hot chilli powder — optional
1½ tsp garam masala
1½ tsp cumin powder
1 tsp coriander powder
1 tsp black pepper
1 tsp kasuri methi, crushed
1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
1 tbsp lemon juice
1 tbsp oil
1 tsp salt
1 tbsp cornflour — helps the exterior become nicely roasted
For finishing
1 tbsp melted butter
½ tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
½ tsp garam masala
1. Prepare the chicken

Wash and pat the chicken completely dry.

Make 3–4 deep cuts through the thickest parts of each leg, right down toward the bone.

This is important. The marinade needs to penetrate the thick thigh meat.

Mix the chicken with the first marinade.

Rub it thoroughly into the cuts.

Refrigerate for at least 1 hour.

3–4 hours is better.

2. Make the second marinade

Mix together:

curd
Kashmiri chilli
hot chilli, if using
garam masala
cumin
coriander
black pepper
kasuri methi
ginger-garlic paste
lemon juice
oil
salt
cornflour

The mixture should be thick, not runny.

Apply generously to the chicken, pushing the marinade into the cuts.

Cover and refrigerate.

Ideally:

8–12 hours overnight.

If you're short on time, give it at least 3 hours.

3. Bring chicken toward room temperature

Take the chicken out of the refrigerator about 30 minutes before cooking.

Don't leave it sitting out for several hours.

4. Prepare the oven

Preheat to:

220°C

Use convection/fan mode if your oven has it.

Place a wire rack over a baking tray.

Lightly oil the rack.

Put the chicken legs on the rack, with space between them.

Don't put them directly on the tray.

The hot air circulating underneath helps roast the chicken instead of steaming it.

5. First bake

Bake at 220°C for 25 minutes.

Then turn the chicken over.

Bake another 15–20 minutes.

At this point, the chicken should be almost cooked.

6. Grill for the char

Switch the oven to GRILL/BROIL.

Move the tray to the upper-middle position, not right against the heating element.

Brush the chicken with:

melted butter + Kashmiri chilli powder + garam masala.

Grill for approximately 3–5 minutes per side.

Watch it closely.

You want dark roasted edges and a few charred spots, but not burnt marinade.

7. Check doneness

The thickest part of the thigh should reach at least:

74°C internal temperature.

If you don't have a thermometer, pierce the thickest part near the bone. The juices should run clear and the meat should no longer be pink.

Because whole legs vary considerably in size, use internal temperature rather than time alone whenever possible.

8. Rest

Remove from the oven and rest for 8–10 minutes.

Don't cut immediately—the juices need time to redistribute.

🔥 For particularly juicy chicken

There's one trick I'd strongly recommend:

Don't overdo the final grilling.

The first 40–45 minutes at 220°C are what cook the meat.

The final grill is only for colour, char and flavour.

If you keep grilling until the chicken looks extremely dark, you'll often end up with beautifully charred skin but dry meat underneath.

My preferred flavour

This marinade gives you something between tandoori chicken and Kerala-style grilled chicken—red, smoky, peppery and mildly tangy.

If you want it less Indian/tandoori and more Western BBQ-style, I can give you a version using garlic, paprika, herbs, mustard and Worcestershire sauce instead.
