---
title: 🧁 Soft Vanilla Cupcakes — 12
---

Ingredients
150 g maida
120 g caster sugar
100 g unsalted butter, softened
2 large eggs, room temperature
80 ml full-fat milk
2 tsp vanilla extract
1½ tsp baking powder
¼ tsp salt

Optional but recommended:

20 g milk powder — gives a lovely bakery-style flavour and softer crumb
1. Prepare

Preheat oven to 170°C conventional mode.

Line a 12-cup muffin tray with cupcake liners.

2. Cream butter and sugar

Beat:

100 g softened butter + 120 g sugar

for 4–5 minutes until pale and fluffy.

This is important for a light cupcake.

3. Add eggs

Beat the eggs together lightly.

Add them gradually, beating well after each addition.

Add the vanilla.

If the mixture looks slightly curdled, don't worry.

4. Add flour and milk

Sift together:

150 g maida
1½ tsp baking powder
¼ tsp salt
20 g milk powder, if using

Add the flour mixture in three additions, alternating with the milk.

Flour → milk → flour → milk → flour

Fold gently.

Stop mixing as soon as the batter is smooth.

5. Fill the cupcakes

Divide the batter between the 12 liners.

Fill each about ⅔ full.

Don't fill to the top — they need room to rise.

6. Bake

Bake at 170°C for 18–22 minutes.

Start checking at 18 minutes.

A toothpick inserted into the centre should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs.

Don't overbake — that's the main reason homemade cupcakes become dry.

7. Cool

Leave them in the tray for 5 minutes, then transfer to a cooling rack.

Allow them to cool completely before frosting.

🍫 Easy chocolate version

If you'd prefer chocolate cupcakes, replace:

25 g of the maida with 25 g unsweetened cocoa powder.

So use:

125 g maida
25 g cocoa powder

Everything else remains the same.

For the chocolate version, I'd also add 1 tbsp hot water or hot milk to the finished batter. It helps bloom the cocoa and gives a richer chocolate flavour.

My preference

For a really good everyday cupcake, I'd make the vanilla version with 20 g milk powder, then top it with a simple buttercream or chocolate ganache rather than an overly sweet frosting.
