---
title: Chilli Chicken
---

Serves 6

Chicken
1 kg boneless chicken, cut into 1-inch pieces
2 tbsp soy sauce
1 tbsp vinegar
1½ tbsp ginger-garlic paste
1 tsp black pepper
1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
1 tsp salt
2 eggs
½ cup cornflour
¼ cup maida
Oil for frying
Sauce
2 tbsp oil
1 tbsp finely chopped garlic
1 tbsp finely chopped ginger
4–6 green chillies, slit
2 tbsp soy sauce
2 tbsp chilli sauce
1 tbsp tomato ketchup
1 tbsp vinegar
1 tsp sugar
¾ cup water or chicken stock
1½ tbsp cornflour + 3 tbsp water
½ tsp black pepper
For the stir-fry
1 large onion, cut into large squares
1 large green capsicum, cut into squares
3–4 spring onions, chopped
1. Marinate the chicken

Mix the chicken with:

soy sauce
vinegar
ginger-garlic paste
black pepper
Kashmiri chilli
salt
2 eggs

Mix very well.

Then add:

½ cup cornflour + ¼ cup maida

You want a thick coating, not a watery batter.

Leave for 20–30 minutes.

2. Fry the chicken

Heat oil to medium-high.

Fry the chicken in batches for approximately 4–5 minutes, until golden and cooked through.

Don't overcrowd the pan.

For especially crisp chilli chicken, fry once, rest for 5 minutes, then flash-fry for another 60–90 seconds.

Keep aside.

3. Make the sauce

Use a large wok or frying pan.

Heat 2 tbsp oil over high heat.

Add:

garlic
ginger
green chillies

Stir-fry for 30–45 seconds.

Add the onion and capsicum.

Don't cook them soft.

Stir-fry on high heat for 1½–2 minutes. They should remain slightly crunchy.

4. Add the sauces

Add:

2 tbsp soy sauce
2 tbsp chilli sauce
1 tbsp ketchup
1 tbsp vinegar
1 tsp sugar
½ tsp black pepper

Stir vigorously for about 30 seconds.

Add ¾ cup water/stock.

Bring to a boil.

5. Thicken

Mix:

1½ tbsp cornflour + 3 tbsp cold water

Add gradually while stirring.

The sauce should become glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon.

Don't make it too thick yet.

The fried chicken will absorb some of the sauce.

6. Add the chicken

Add all the fried chicken.

Toss aggressively over high heat for 1–2 minutes.

Add the spring onions.

Turn off the heat.

⭐ The balance I recommend

For the style I think you'll like:

Salty → savoury → mildly sweet → spicy → slightly tangy.

Don't make it predominantly sweet. The 1 tsp sugar + 1 tbsp ketchup is enough.

If you want it hotter, increase the green chillies rather than adding lots of chilli powder.

For a proper restaurant-style finish

Right at the end, add:

½ tsp freshly ground black pepper + 1 tsp sesame oil

and toss through.

The sesame oil is optional, but gives that unmistakable Indo-Chinese restaurant aroma.

If you want Chilli Chicken with gravy

Use 1½ cups stock/water instead of ¾ cup, and increase the cornflour slurry to:

2 tbsp cornflour + 4 tbsp water.

Then add the fried chicken and simmer only 1–2 minutes.

For dry chilli chicken, stick with the recipe above.

What I'd serve with it

For 6 adults:

1 kg chilli chicken
750 g uncooked basmati rice for fried rice, or
2–3 packs/portions of Hakka noodles
Cucumber/onion salad on the side

If the chilli chicken itself is the star, dry chilli chicken + egg/chicken fried rice would be my choice.
