---
title: Soft Vanilla Sponge Cake
---

Makes: 1 × 8-inch cake
Serves: 8–10

Ingredients
200 g maida
150 g caster sugar
150 g unsalted butter, softened
3 large eggs, room temperature
100 ml full-fat milk
2 tsp vanilla extract
2 tsp baking powder
¼ tsp salt
Optional for extra softness
2 tbsp milk powder
1. Prepare the oven

Preheat to 170°C, conventional mode.

Grease an 8-inch round cake tin and line the bottom with baking paper.

Don't grease the sides excessively — this helps the cake climb.

2. Cream butter and sugar

Put the softened butter and sugar into a bowl.

Beat for 4–5 minutes until:

pale
light
fluffy

This step is important. Don't rush it.

3. Add the eggs

Beat the eggs separately.

Add them to the butter mixture a little at a time, beating well after each addition.

If the mixture looks slightly curdled, don't worry.

Add the vanilla.

4. Add the dry ingredients

Sift together:

maida
baking powder
salt
milk powder, if using

Add the dry mixture to the butter/egg mixture in 3 additions, alternating with the milk.

For example:

flour → milk → flour → milk → flour

Fold gently.

Don't overmix.

Stop as soon as there are no visible streaks of flour.

5. Bake

Transfer to the prepared tin.

Tap the tin once or twice gently on the counter to remove large air pockets.

Bake at:

170°C for approximately 35–40 minutes.

Start checking at 33 minutes.

Insert a toothpick into the centre.

It should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs.

6. Cool

Leave the cake in the tin for 10 minutes.

Run a knife around the edge.

Turn it out onto a cooling rack.

Allow it to cool completely before cutting.

⭐ For a really soft sponge

Once cooled, you can brush it with a simple syrup:

50 g sugar
60 ml water

Heat until the sugar dissolves, then cool.

Brush lightly over the cake.

This is particularly useful if you're going to fill it with whipped cream or use it as a birthday cake.
