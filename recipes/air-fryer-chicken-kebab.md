---
title: 🍗 Air-Fryer Chicken Kebab
---

1 kg chicken | 6 adults

This gives you a tender, lightly charred, tandoori-style kebab without needing an oven or much oil.

Chicken
1 kg boneless chicken thighs, cut into 1½-inch pieces
150 g thick curd
1½ tbsp ginger-garlic paste
1½ tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder
½–1 tsp hot chilli powder
1½ tsp garam masala
1 tsp cumin powder
1 tsp coriander powder
1 tsp black pepper
1 tsp kasuri methi, crushed
1 tbsp lemon juice
1½ tsp salt
1 tbsp oil
1 tbsp cornflour
1 tbsp besan — optional, but gives a better roasted coating
Optional for serving
Lemon wedges
Sliced onion
Mint chutney
1. Prepare the chicken

Cut the chicken into fairly large pieces. Don't make them too small or they'll dry out.

Pat the chicken dry.

Mix everything together thoroughly.

The marinade should be thick enough to cling to the chicken rather than run off.

Marinating time

Minimum: 2 hours

Best: 6–12 hours refrigerated.

If marinating overnight, don't add extra lemon juice beyond the amount above—the acid can affect the texture if excessive.

2. Before air frying

Take the chicken out of the refrigerator about 20–30 minutes before cooking.

Preheat your air fryer to:

200°C for 5 minutes

Lightly brush or spray the basket with oil.

Arrange the chicken pieces in one layer.

Don't crowd them.

3. Air fry
At 200°C:

8 minutes

Then open the basket and turn/shake the chicken.

Brush very lightly with oil.

Continue:

6–8 minutes at 200°C

So you're looking at approximately 14–16 minutes total.

4. Get the kebab char

For the final 2 minutes, increase to:

210–220°C

if your air fryer allows it.

This gives you those lovely browned/charred edges.

Keep an eye on them because the curd marinade can darken quickly.

🌡️ Don't rely only on time

The chicken is done when the thickest pieces reach:

74°C internally.

If your pieces are particularly large, they may need another 2–4 minutes.

⭐ One trick for really juicy kebabs

After air frying, don't immediately cut or serve them.

Leave them in the basket for 3–4 minutes with the drawer open.

Then squeeze a little lemon over them just before serving.

If you want them more like restaurant kebabs

I'd add 1 tbsp roasted besan to the marinade and use chicken thigh.

The combination of:

curd + besan + cornflour + Kashmiri chilli + high heat

gives a much better crust than curd and spices alone.

And I wouldn't put vegetables between the chicken pieces.

For air-fryer kebabs, chicken pieces alone brown much better. If you want onion/capsicum, cook them separately for a few minutes and serve alongside.

Best serving: chicken kebab + mint chutney + onion rings + lemon.
