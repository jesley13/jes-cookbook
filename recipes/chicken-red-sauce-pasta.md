---
title: Chicken Red Sauce Pasta
---

750 g pasta | 1 kg chicken | 6 generous servings
Ingredients
Pasta
750 g Borges Durum Wheat Penne Rigate
5–6 litres water
2 tbsp salt
Chicken
1 kg boneless chicken, cut into small bite-sized pieces
1½ tsp salt
1½ tsp black pepper
1½ tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
1½ tsp Italian seasoning
1½ tbsp olive oil
Red sauce
1 kg ripe tomatoes
3 medium onions, finely chopped
12 cloves garlic, finely chopped
3 tbsp tomato paste
1½ tsp Italian seasoning
1 tsp chilli flakes
1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
1 tsp sugar only if the tomatoes are acidic
2 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp butter
Salt to taste
1–2 tbsp tomato ketchup — optional
Cheese

Use the Amul mozzarella + cheddar blend you showed me:

150–175 g

I'd use 150 g initially.

1. Prepare the tomatoes

For fresh tomatoes, I recommend blanching them.

Make a small cross on each tomato.

Put them into boiling water for 2–3 minutes, then transfer to cold water.

Peel and blend.

You should get roughly 800–900 ml tomato puree from 1 kg tomatoes.

Don't add water while blending.

2. Season and cook the chicken

Mix the chicken with:

1½ tsp salt
1½ tsp pepper
1½ tsp Kashmiri chilli
1½ tsp Italian seasoning

Heat 1½ tbsp oil in a large pan.

Cook the chicken in batches over medium-high heat for about 6–8 minutes, until lightly browned and cooked through.

Remove and keep aside.

3. Cook the pasta

Bring 5–6 litres of water to a vigorous boil.

Add 2 tbsp salt.

Add the 750 g Borges penne.

Start tasting it 2 minutes before the packet's recommended cooking time.

You want it al dente.

Before draining, reserve at least 2 cups of pasta water.

Drain.

Don't rinse.

4. Make the red sauce

Use the same large pan.

Add:

2 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp butter

Add the chopped onions.

Cook on medium heat for 7–8 minutes, until soft and beginning to turn golden.

Add the garlic.

Cook for 1–2 minutes.

Add:

3 tbsp tomato paste
1½ tsp Italian seasoning
1 tsp chilli flakes
1 tsp black pepper

Cook for 1 minute.

5. Add the tomatoes

Add the tomato puree.

Add 1 tsp salt initially.

Simmer uncovered for 20–25 minutes.

This is important.

You want the sauce to become thick, rich and concentrated, not like tomato soup.

Taste.

If it's acidic:

Add ½–1 tsp sugar.

If the tomatoes are already sweet:

Don't add sugar.

And given the problem you had with the previous red sauce being too sweet and spicy, I would skip the ketchup initially. Only add 1 tbsp if the finished sauce needs a little more rounded flavour.

6. Add the chicken

Return the chicken to the sauce.

Simmer for 5 minutes.

Taste again.

At this stage, correct the seasoning before adding the pasta.

You want:

savoury → tomato-rich → mildly spicy → slightly tangy

not sweet.

7. Add the pasta

Add all 750 g cooked penne.

Add ½ cup pasta water.

Toss over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes.

Add more pasta water, a little at a time, until the sauce coats the penne nicely.

Don't make it dry.

The pasta will continue absorbing the sauce after you turn off the heat.

8. Add the cheese

Turn the heat to low.

Add:

150 g grated/diced Amul mozzarella-cheddar.

Toss gently until just melted.

If it becomes too thick, add a splash of pasta water.

You can increase the cheese to 175 g if you want it cheesier.

⭐ My recommended final proportions
Borges penne | 750 g
Chicken | 1 kg
Fresh tomatoes | 1 kg
Onion | 3 medium
Garlic | 12 cloves
Tomato paste | 3 tbsp
Amul mozzarella/cheddar | 150–175 g
Olive oil | 3½ tbsp
Butter | 1 tbsp

Don't add sugar, ketchup or lots of chilli at the beginning
Fresh tomatoes vary enormously. Cook the sauce properly, and then decide what it needs. That will avoid the sweet-and-spicy problem you encountered previously.
For this version, I'd also keep the flavour garlicky, tomato-rich and moderately peppery rather than heavily Italian-seasoned. The chicken, tomato and cheese should remain the dominant flavours
