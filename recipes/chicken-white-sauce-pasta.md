## Ingredients

### Chicken
- 500 g chicken breast, diced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

### Pasta
- 750 g Borges Durum Wheat Penne Rigate
- 1 tbsp salt (for boiling water)

### Sauce
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 cups milk (room temperature)
- 0.5 cup parmesan cheese, grated
- 1 tsp oregano
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Boil water in a large pot, add 1 tbsp salt, and cook the penne pasta until al dente. Drain and set aside.
2. In a frying pan, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add the diced chicken breast, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Cook until the chicken is browned and cooked through (about 7-8 minutes). Remove from pan and set aside.
3. In the same pan, melt the butter over medium-low heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute until lightly golden.
4. Gradually whisk in the milk, ensuring there are no lumps. Cook for 3-5 minutes until the sauce thickens.
5. Stir in the parmesan cheese, oregano, salt, and black pepper.
6. Add the cooked chicken and pasta into the sauce. Toss well to combine and heat through for another 2 minutes.
7. Serve hot.

## My Notes

- Do not use fresh cream. The milk and butter are enough.
- Avoid adding mushrooms, capsicum, or other vegetables. Keep it simple.
- 500 g pasta wasn't enough. Use 750 g next time.

## What I Would Change Next Time

- Try adding a pinch of nutmeg to the white sauce for extra flavor.
