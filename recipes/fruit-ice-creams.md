---
title: Fruit Ice Creams
---

BASE RECIPE
1 serving (78 calories): 
► Frozen banana slices (1 medium banana, 90 g)
► Unsweetened almond milk (1.5 tsp, 7.5 ml)

4 servings (310 calories): 
► Frozen banana slices (4 medium bananas, 360 g)
► Unsweetened almond milk (2 tbsp, 30 ml)

--------------------

#1 - BERRY BANANA ICE CREAM 
(449 calories total, or 112 calories per 120 ml serving)

► Frozen banana slices (4 medium bananas, 360 g)
► Unsweetened almond milk (2 tbsp, 30 ml)
► Pure vanilla extract (1 tsp, 5 ml)
► Maple syrup (1 tbsp, 15 ml)
► Frozen mixed berries (1 cup, 140 g)

--------------------

#2 - CHOCOLATE PEANUT BUTTER BANANA ICE CREAM
(499 calories total, or 125 calories per 120 ml serving)

► Frozen banana slices (4 medium bananas, 360 g)
► Unsweetened almond milk (2 tbsp, 30 ml)
► Pure vanilla extract (1 tsp, 5 ml)
► Maple syrup (1 tbsp, 15 ml)
► Cocoa powder (3 tbsp, 15 g)
► Natural peanut butter (1 tbsp, 15 g)
► Pinch of sea salt

--------------------

#3 - TROPICAL BANANA ICE CREAM
(449 calories total, or 112 calories per 120 ml serving)

► Frozen banana slices (4 medium bananas, 360 g)
► Unsweetened almond milk (2 tbsp, 30 ml)
► Pure vanilla extract (1 tsp, 5 ml)
► Maple syrup (1 tbsp, 15 ml)
► Frozen pineapple and mango chunks (1 cup, 140 g)

--------------------

#4 - COFFEE & BANANA ICE CREAM
(382 calories total, or 96 calories per 120 ml serving)

► Frozen banana slices (4 medium bananas, 360 g)
► Unsweetened almond milk (2 tbsp, 30 ml)
► Pure vanilla extract (1 tsp, 5 ml)
► Maple syrup (1 tbsp, 15 ml)
► Instant coffee powder (2 tsp, 10 g)
► Cocoa powder (1 tsp, 2 g)
► Pinch of sea salt
