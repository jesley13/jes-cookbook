---
title: 🥩Kerala Beef Biriyani
---

Serves 6 generously
Ingredients
Beef
1 kg beef, cut into 1½-inch pieces
1½ tsp salt
½ tsp turmeric
1½ tbsp ginger-garlic paste
2 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
1½ tsp coriander powder
1 tsp black pepper
1 tsp garam masala
2 green chillies, slit
1 medium onion, sliced
½ cup water
For the masala
3 large onions, thinly sliced
2 medium tomatoes, chopped
2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
4–5 green chillies, slit
2 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped
2 tbsp mint leaves, chopped
2 tbsp ghee
3 tbsp oil
1 tsp turmeric
1½ tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
1 tsp coriander powder
1 tsp garam masala
1 tsp black pepper
Salt to taste
Whole spices
2-inch cinnamon
5–6 cloves
4 cardamoms
2 bay leaves
1 star anise
1 tsp fennel seeds
Rice
750 g good-quality basmati rice
4–5 litres water
2 tbsp salt
1 cinnamon stick
4 cloves
3 cardamoms
1 bay leaf
1 tsp lemon juice
For layering
3 tbsp ghee
½ cup fried onions
2 tbsp chopped coriander
2 tbsp chopped mint
¼ tsp saffron soaked in 3 tbsp warm milk — optional
1 tbsp rose water — optional
1. Pressure-cook the beef

Put into the pressure cooker:

beef
salt
turmeric
ginger-garlic paste
Kashmiri chilli
coriander powder
black pepper
garam masala
green chillies
sliced onion
½ cup water

Mix thoroughly.

Pressure cooking

Full flame until the first whistle.

Then:

Medium flame for 15–20 minutes.

Switch off and allow the pressure to release naturally.

The beef should be tender but not falling apart.

Keep the cooking liquid.

2. Make the beef masala

Heat a large heavy-bottomed pot.

Add:

3 tbsp oil + 2 tbsp ghee

Add the whole spices:

cinnamon
cloves
cardamom
bay leaf
star anise
fennel

Fry for about 30 seconds.

Add the 3 sliced onions.

Cook on medium heat for 10–12 minutes, until properly golden.

Don't rush this. The browned onions are important to the biriyani.

Add:

ginger-garlic paste
green chillies

Fry for 2 minutes.

Add the chopped tomatoes.

Cook until they become completely soft and the oil starts separating.

3. Add the spices

Add:

turmeric
Kashmiri chilli powder
coriander powder
garam masala
black pepper

Fry for about 30 seconds.

Add the cooked beef.

Mix thoroughly.

Now add ½–1 cup of the reserved beef cooking liquid.

Cook uncovered for 8–10 minutes.

You want a thick, intensely flavoured beef masala.

Don't leave it watery.

When you put rice over it, excess liquid will make the biriyani mushy.

4. Cook the basmati rice

Wash 750 g basmati rice several times until the water is reasonably clear.

Soak for 20–30 minutes.

Bring 4–5 litres water to a vigorous boil.

Add:

2 tbsp salt
cinnamon
cloves
cardamom
bay leaf
lemon juice

Drain the soaked rice and add it.

Cook until the rice is approximately 70–75% cooked.

The grain should be:

soft outside but still noticeably firm in the centre.

This is critical.

Drain immediately.

5. Layer the biriyani

Keep the beef masala at the bottom of the heavy pot.

Spread the partially cooked rice over it.

Don't stir.

Top with:

3 tbsp ghee
fried onions
coriander
mint
saffron milk
optional rose water

If you have extra fried onions, save some for serving.

6. Dum cooking

Cover the pot very tightly.

If the lid doesn't seal well, place aluminium foil over the pot and then put the lid on.

Heat:

Medium-high for 3–4 minutes

Then:

Very low heat for 20–25 minutes.

Switch off.

Do not open immediately.

Let it rest for 10–15 minutes.

7. Mix carefully

Don't attack the biriyani with a spoon and stir everything together.

Take a large flat spoon and gently lift from the bottom, bringing some beef and masala through the rice.

You want some areas of white rice, some masala-coated rice and plenty of beef distributed throughout.

⭐ The ratios I'd use

For six hungry adults:

1 kg beef : 750 g basmati rice

is what I'd recommend.

If you want a particularly beef-heavy biriyani, go:

1.2 kg beef : 750 g rice.

I wouldn't increase the rice to 1 kg unless you're expecting 8+ people or specifically want leftovers.

What makes this biriyani good

The key isn't loading it with spices. It's:

Tender pressure-cooked beef
Properly browned onions
Thick beef masala
Rice cooked only 70–75% before dum
Enough ghee
Gentle dum cooking
Serve with
Raita
Pickled onion
Lime pickle
Pappadam
A little coconut chutney

For a Kerala-style spread, I'd particularly recommend beef biriyani + cucumber/onion raita + pappadam + lime pickle.
