---
title: 🍗 Crispy Chicken Winglets
---

Quantity: 1 kg winglets
Style: Juicy inside, crisp outside, Indian/Kerala-spiced
Method: Brine → marinate → coat → air fry

1. Brine — 2 to 4 hours

Mix:

1 litre cold water
45 g salt (about 2½ tbsp)
1 tbsp sugar — optional
½ tsp black pepper
2–3 garlic cloves, lightly crushed — optional

Add 1 kg winglets, cover and refrigerate for 2–4 hours.

Then:

Drain.
Rinse very briefly with cold water.
Pat completely dry with paper towels.

Important: Because the chicken is brined, do not add salt to the marinade.

2. Spice marinade

Mix:

1½ tbsp ginger-garlic paste
1½ tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
½ tsp hot chilli powder
1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
1 tsp cumin powder
1 tsp coriander powder
1 tsp garam masala
1 tbsp lemon juice
2 tbsp thick curd
1 tbsp oil
No additional salt

Coat the winglets thoroughly.

Refrigerate for 1–2 hours.

3. Crispy coating

Mix:

½ cup cornflour
¼ cup rice flour
1 tbsp maida
½ tsp black pepper
½ tsp Kashmiri chilli powder

Take each marinated winglet and coat well.

Shake off excess coating.

Don't make the coating wet or pasty. You want a relatively dry, thin layer.

4. Air Fry
Preheat

200°C for 5 minutes.

Lightly brush or spray the basket with oil.

Arrange winglets in one layer. Don't overcrowd them.

Lightly spray/brush the winglets with oil.

Cook

200°C — 10 minutes

Open and turn each piece.

Lightly spray with oil again if the coating looks dry.

200°C — another 8–10 minutes

Then:

210–220°C — 2–3 minutes

for deeper browning and crispness.

The thickest piece should reach 74°C internally.

5. Optional Kerala finishing — recommended

For a stronger Kerala character, heat:

1 tbsp coconut oil
5–6 garlic cloves, chopped
2 green chillies, slit
1 sprig curry leaves
½ tsp freshly crushed black pepper

Fry for 30–45 seconds, then add the cooked winglets.

Toss on high heat for 1–2 minutes.

Don't add sauce or water.

Final result

Brining → juicy meat
Curd + spices → flavour and tenderness
Cornflour + rice flour → crisp crust
Air fryer high heat → browned exterior without deep frying
Coconut oil + curry leaves + pepper → Kerala finish
